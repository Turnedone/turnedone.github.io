Robotics

Warehouse delivery robot

A TurtleBot 4 takes delivery orders from a queue and drives them from shelves to packing stations through a simulated warehouse with keep-out zones, slow zones and a moving forklift, and docks to charge before an order it could not finish. Built on the industry stack: Nav2, a C++ mission layer on BehaviorTree.CPP, end-to-end tests and KPIs computed from recorded runs.

  • C++17
  • ROS 2 Jazzy
  • Nav2
  • Gazebo
  • Behavior trees
